# CHP_examples

Results and figures for the multi-objective design and control of multigeneration (combined heat and power) systems for a residential building complex, showing compromise designs that trade off cost, water consumption, and greenhouse gas emissions.

## Contents
- `plots/`: data files (`.dat`) and figures (`.pdf`) for electric and thermal loads, temperatures, hot water storage, costs, emissions, and water use, and `plotfile1.jl` to make the figures
