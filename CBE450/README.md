# CBE450

Julia/JuMP notebooks for the UW–Madison process design course CBE 450 (Fall 2017 and Spring 2018): flash, Gibbs reactor, and NH₃ flowsheet models and their optimization, and textbook examples 16.1–16.4.

## Contents
- `Fall2017/`, `Spring2018/`: one folder per semester, each with three sessions
  - `SessionI/`: installing packages, simple JuMP models, flash, Gibbs reactor, and NH₃ flowsheet models
  - `SessionII/`: optimization of the Session I models
  - `SessionIII/`: textbook examples 16.1, 16.3, and 16.4 and loops in JuMP
